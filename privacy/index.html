Privacy

Effective August 19, 2026

This notice explains what Embla collects through the waitlist at emblahealth.com, why we collect it, and the choices available to you.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist, we collect your email address. If you use the questionnaire, we also collect the answers you choose about:

  • where you are in your health journey, including whether you are recovering after a baby;
  • what feels hardest right now;
  • whether you want medication tracking; and
  • the referral or campaign source in the link you used, when present.

Our hosting and email providers may also process ordinary technical information such as IP address, browser type, and request logs to deliver and secure their services.

How we use it

We use waitlist information to send product-launch and early-access messages, understand which groups Embla should serve first, measure which outreach is useful, and protect the waitlist from abuse.

Who processes it

We use service providers, including our website host and email-list provider, to operate the waitlist. They may process information only to provide those services to us. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your email address with advertisers.

How long we keep it

We keep waitlist information while we prepare and launch Embla, or until you ask us to remove it. We may retain limited records when reasonably necessary for security, legal, or unsubscribe obligations.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe using the link in any email. You can also ask to access, correct, or delete your waitlist information by emailing hello@embla.app.

Changes

If this notice changes materially, we will update the effective date and post the revised version here.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to hello@embla.app.

Embla is a tracking tool, not a substitute for your GP, prescriber, or care team.
